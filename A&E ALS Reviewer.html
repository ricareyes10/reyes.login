⛵ The Wayfinder Theme & Navigation Protocol

HOW TO COMPLETE THE READING QUEST

Ancient wayfinders read the ocean stars, swells, and winds to navigate islands. As an ALS Voyager, you will navigate through functional reading tasks to reach mastery!

🔤

1. Vocabulary in Context

Decode unfamiliar terms by looking at nearby context clues inside the community passages and notices.

📊

2. Graph & Text Inference

Analyze community tables, fish catch charts, and numerical survey data to draw accurate logical conclusions.

📖

3. Story Analysis

Identify main ideas, underlying themes, character motives, and ecological solutions across stories.

💡 Voyage Rules & Teacher Monitoring: Limited Hint Allowance
  • Limited Hints: Maui's Fishhook Hints are strictly limited! You get 1 use per question and a maximum of 3 total hint uses per quest. Use them wisely!
  • Heart Protection: You start with 3 Hearts. Incorrect choices or timeouts remove 1 Heart.
  • Teacher Score Tracking: Teacher Studio records all quest scores, accuracy percentages, and streak records so your teacher can monitor student progress.
